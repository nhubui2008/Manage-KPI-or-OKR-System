# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Managers, department leads, HR/admin users, and employees use this system during daily business operations to plan work, assign responsibility, follow execution, and monitor progress across KPI, OKR, and project workstreams.

## Product Purpose

The product helps organizations connect goals with execution. Success means users can see what needs attention, move work forward quickly, record operational context, and trust that KPI/OKR data and Kanban task activity stay aligned.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and work-focused. The interface should feel calm and professional, with enough density for repeated operational use.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style layouts, decorative dashboards, oversized cards, vague AI-generated copy, hidden workflow actions, and screens that force users to scroll or scan excessively before finding blocked, overdue, or assigned work.

## Design Principles

- Put operational risk first: blocked, overdue, urgent, and assigned work should be easy to find.
- Keep workflows compact: prefer dense but readable controls over large decorative panels.
- Preserve familiar product UI patterns: Bootstrap controls, predictable modals, clear buttons, and direct feedback.
- Make AI assistance reviewable: generated tasks and comments should be easy to inspect before users act.
- Keep KPI/OKR integrity intact while improving the operational layer around it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-reachable controls, readable Vietnamese labels, visible focus states, and reduced-motion alternatives for UI feedback.
